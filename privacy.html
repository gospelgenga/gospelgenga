Privacy

Privacy policy

We make Bible animation. We don't run ads on this site, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you.

Last updated 11 October 2026. GospelGenga runs the YouTube channel @GospelGenga and this website, gospelgenga.com. Questions: gospelgenga@gmail.com.

This website

You can read every page without an account. We don't set cookies, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts of our own. A few services are involved in delivering the page, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to run and protect its service (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Fonts. The typefaces come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them and Google receives your IP address (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Giving. The donation panel on the home page is Ko-fi's own widget. Ko-fi and its payment providers handle any gift, and Ko-fi may set its own cookies when the panel loads (Ko-fi Privacy Policy). We see only what Ko-fi shows creators, such as the name and message you choose to leave.
  • Links. YouTube, Instagram, Patreon and Ko-fi open on their own sites, where their policies apply.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we never pass them on.

Our production tools and YouTube data

GospelGenga Studio is the private tool our team uses to plan and finish episodes. It is not offered to the public.

GospelGenga Studio uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API) to read the statistics of our own YouTube channel. It asks Google for read-only access only, so it cannot upload, edit, delete or post anything. By using these services it is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of the data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it reads

Totals for our channel's own videos: views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, traffic sources, the search terms that led viewers to our videos, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate. YouTube reports these as aggregated statistics; they don't identify individual viewers. The tool reads no one's account and no other channel's statistics. To show which outside videos YouTube recommends ours next to, it looks up only those videos' public titles and channel names, as anyone can see them on YouTube.

How we use and keep it

Only to decide what to make next and to improve our titles, thumbnails and storytelling. The data is stored on our own production computer. We don't sell it, share it, or combine it with any data about individual people.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time from Google's security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions. From then on it can read nothing new, and we delete the copies it stored within 7 days.

Children

Our videos are made for all ages. This site doesn't ask anyone, child or adult, for personal information.

Changes

If anything here changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
